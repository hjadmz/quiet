{% comment %} Build-time table of contents from h2 headings. Pure Liquid, no JS. Rendered only when a post sets `toc: true` in its front matter. Usage: {% include toc.html html=content %} h2 only, and that is the whole design of it. A contents list answers "what shape is this post, and where do I jump in" — which h2s answer; h3s are subdivisions you meet inside a section, not destinations you pick from the top. Measured, including them gave 16 entries filling 63% of a phone's first screen against 6 entries and 28%. It also deletes the nesting logic, so there is no open-list bookkeeping left to get wrong. {% endcomment %} {%- assign _chunks = include.html | split: '' | first -%} {%- assign _id_parts = _tag | split: ' id="' -%} {%- if _id_parts.size > 1 %}{% assign _has_toc = true %}{% endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- if _has_toc -%}
